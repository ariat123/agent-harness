# Canonical architecture

The canonical product is `maps_lead_research/`, invoked with `python -m maps_lead_research`. Its campaign contract is v2; SQLite registry migration 1 and export schema v2 are independent versioned contracts. Compatibility runners retain their original schemas and behavior. No legacy database or historical output is migrated in place.

## Stages and ownership

1. **Configuration** (`config.py`): strict JSON validation, sensible zero-spend defaults, category/queries, geography, review/contact/email limits, request/credit/page ceilings, reserve, role priorities, resume/refresh policy, paths. No downstream ICP definition. Unknown fields fail validation.
2. **Geography** (`geography.py`): versioned US city plan with curated major/secondary markets followed by expansion through the bundled Census places catalog. Every state plus DC is represented. Regions and states interleave before deeper tiers; explicit city overrides come first. Nationwide expansion never queries all ZIPs. States and Census regions expand automatically; cities and ZIPs use explicit targets. Non-US campaigns require explicit cities and provider country corroboration.
3. **Maps** (`providers.py`, `pipeline.py`): RapidAPI Maps Data via the existing core single-page transport. One request at a time, configurable interval, bounded retries, country/language, and breadth-first page expansion. Single-worker concurrency is deliberately within provider limits. Every attempt—including retries, failures and uncertain outcomes—consumes the request ceiling.
4. **Location normalization**: reuse the existing Maps parser, preserving zero reviews/coordinates, business status, place, address/geography, ratings, website/domain/phone, Maps URL, query/target, timestamp and raw pointer. Closed records are retained for reconciliation rather than silently filtered by the legacy `api_items` helper.
5. **Historical suppression**: configured suppression CSV is snapshotted into the run. The shared registry represents previously discovered companies, even when research is incomplete. No name-only suppression. Registry history also supplies the enrichment-only backlog, filtered by the current campaign’s review/geography rules and explicit niche evidence before spending.
6. **Deduplication** (`registry.py`): exact Place ID, normalized registrable/tenant domain, valid normalized phone, then normalized name plus full address. Domain parsing uses tldextract's bundled PSL, including private suffixes, with all network refresh/cache disabled. Common shared social/directory/email hosts are not company keys. Contradictory authoritative keys go to an identity-conflict exclusion/quarantine; no silent bridging merge.
7. **Company resolution**: stable hashed company/location/contact IDs; domains and phones have their own records; company memberships retain campaign provenance. Source-backed parent relationships have an explicit dated table/API. Identity lookup follows an unambiguous parent relationship, while retaining original company IDs and source rows; cycles and ambiguous relationships fail closed. The first qualifying location admits a company; later locations do not consume the net-new target. Extra results in the last provider page are retained as documented capacity exclusions.
8. **Website research**: one shared evidence shape for existing bounded direct-HTTP primitives and Firecrawl HTML/Markdown. Homepage, then the best discovered official-domain leadership/team/management/about/contact/location page; the default is at most two Firecrawl operations and one HTTP page per domain. No crawling, search API, paid map endpoint or speculative URL blasting. Higher page ceilings are explicit. Company breadth precedes depth.
9. **People** (`extract.py`): official-site schema.org Person records and bounded explicit name/title pairs; supported role priorities; former/retired evidence rejected. Preserve exact title, confirming URL, biography/professional URL when available, evidence date and excerpt. Deterministic extraction is intentionally conservative and does not promise a contact on every site.
10. **Emails**: official-domain public email candidates and free-hosted business addresses explicitly presented as contacts on the official site; named-person association when supported by a unique name/pattern match; general/unassigned public business emails remain general. Pattern inference requires two named public examples supporting exactly one company pattern and a source-backed target person. Provenance is independent of deliverability. `catch_all_domain` is a supported output vocabulary for downstream evidence, never inferred by this scraper. No DNS or mailbox checks masquerade as verification; verifier calls do not exist in the canonical code.
11. **Persistent registry**: SQLite companies, identities, locations, domains, phones, appearances, research status/evidence/failure, contacts, emails, source artifacts/rows, relationships, first/last seen dates. A registry filesystem lock serializes writes/campaign discovery to make company admission and cross-campaign grouping deterministic. Batch discovers sequentially, then enriches sequentially under the shared account lock.
12. **Exports and QA** (`outputs.py`): stable headers even for empty outputs, atomic writes, spreadsheet formula escaping, exhaustive observation dispositions, provider attempts and per-domain cap validation, person/email evidence checks and explicit research gaps. Raw data remains source-faithful except credential redaction. Export is offline.
13. **Resume/rebuild**: request-first durable receipts, immutable campaigns, persisted account/domain operation counts, source evidence reuse, separate backlog, and isolated offline rebuild. A new output folder is required for changed budgets/configuration. Committed per-page interpretation receipts freeze company snapshots and historical-match decisions, so later campaigns cannot alter an older run’s exports or rebuild. Raw payload hashes detect changed evidence.

## Accounting and locking

All canonical Firecrawl campaigns in this checkout share `.state/firecrawl-account.json` and its OS lock, regardless of registry/output paths. The lock covers the enrichment queue. Account balance is read before and after operations, including a zero balance; unavailable balance fails closed. Starting/ending balance and provider-reported credits are retained. An operation reserves one credit before execution; budget consumption is the greater of accumulated conservative reservations/reported credits and starting minus latest balance. Replenishment or delayed balance reporting cannot reopen already-consumed budget. Reserve and maximum delta are checked before each operation. If a provider reports unexpected extra cost or external spending crosses a threshold, processing stops and reports the breach.

The request selects `proxy: basic`, HTML/Markdown, `parsers: []`, and no actions or JSON/LLM extraction. This keeps the supported scrape operation bounded under the documented basic pricing, avoiding automatic proxy/PDF cost expansion. Provider contracts were checked against [Firecrawl scrape documentation](https://docs.firecrawl.dev/api-reference/endpoint/scrape) and [credit usage documentation](https://docs.firecrawl.dev/api-reference/endpoint/credit-usage) on 2026-09-08. Balance and scrape were **not called live** during development.

A local lock coordinates this canonical checkout. Other hosts, copies of the repository, the Firecrawl dashboard, and old campaign scripts cannot be forced to honor it. Run one canonical queue for the account and retire direct legacy paid execution. Live polling detects outside consumption but cannot prevent outside spend or retroactively guarantee a provider's billing contract. No paid campaign was used to validate the implementation.

## Failure and recovery

Maps receipts transition `pending` → `success` or known HTTP failure. Raw responses are saved before the success commit. Resume recovers successful raw responses without resending; completed/empty pages are skipped. 429/selected 5xx failures can retry within the original attempt ceiling and retry policy. Unknown network outcomes remain pending and block that search; status/export show the unresolved attempt. Other independent searches may proceed within the hard ceiling.

Firecrawl reservations transition `pending` → `received` → `success`. If the raw response was saved before a crash, the next run recovers it and polls balance without another scrape. A pending operation without a preserved definitive response blocks all canonical account spending. Do not delete its reservation or change it to success based on elapsed time. Recover provider evidence/request outcome first; if that cannot be established, leave the operation blocked and inspect the account manually. This is a deliberate limit to avoid accidental duplicate billing: the provider does not offer an exactly-once transaction shared with local storage.

Known Firecrawl 429 failures can retry, but each attempt still consumes the per-domain operation count and a conservative reservation. Other errors produce backlog; 402 and uncertain failures stop paid research. A two-operation domain limit may yield fewer than two successful pages. Resume never resets those counts. Explicitly authorized refresh uses a new run. HTTP errors are also preserved; no unbounded retry loop.

Offline rebuild reads the immutable baseline registry snapshot, campaign/target/suppression snapshots, Maps receipts/raw payloads, and per-domain research receipt pointers/raw pages. It reconstructs into a new directory and a disposable registry, never overwriting the live registry or source run. Reused domain evidence carries source paths and a local evidence snapshot. It does not need exported CSVs or the aggregate state.json and never creates a provider client. Preserve raw evidence alongside the run for portability.

## Historical migration

`import-history` accepts directories, CSVs and legacy databases; it recursively inventories CSV/DB sources, opens `leads` tables read-only and records file hashes, row provenance, confidence and import reports. CSV fields are adapted using documented aliases in `registry.py`; invalid/insufficient identity and conflicting keys are quarantined in `source_rows`. Email-only/verifier tables are not authoritative companies. Changed files are new source artifacts; unchanged files are skipped. All input rows remain traceable; no source is overwritten.

Supported preserved JSON page bodies from generalized HTTP/Firecrawl and campaign adapters can seed reusable research. A manifest or receipt claiming completion alone is insufficient. Imported emails remain unverified, regardless of a legacy field named `verified`. Full legacy fields remain in source rows for a separately reviewed verifier-data migration. Unsupported JSON formats remain untouched and are counted in the report; their related company CSV/DB rows still enter the pending backlog. Import confidence is identity/evidence confidence, not business-fit or mailbox confidence.

## Practical limits

The bundled geography catalog covers 31,847 Census places across 51 jurisdictions. Curated major/secondary markets run first, then additional places interleave by region/state until the request ceiling can be filled. The pinned source is the [2025 Census National Places Gazetteer](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.2025.html); source URL and SHA-256 are stored in `data/us_places_2025.json`. Planning is entirely offline. Full-catalog exhaustion or exhausted configured pages before a very large target is reported. `catalog.py` can rebuild the catalog from the pinned downloaded ZIP without networking. The target is an upper bound on net-new source-eligible companies, not a promise of 1,000 named contacts. Google Maps pages can repeat, omit fields or spill outside requested geography; unknown required review/geography fields become documented exclusions. Exact name+address matching intentionally avoids fuzzy nationwide merging.

Source relevance is conservatively confirmed only by explicit matching service evidence. Unknown relevance becomes a gap and is never treated as final ICP qualification. Narrow searches such as commercial signs retain the requested terms; broad property management records service mix including SFR rather than disqualifying it. Company summary is an evidence excerpt, not an invented description. Evidence dates describe when the source was observed; historical evidence is reused unless refresh is explicitly requested.

The CLI is usable without a Codex skill. The skill only translates input, validates/executes these commands with explicit paid authorization, monitors/resumes and links artifacts; operational mechanics remain in Python.
