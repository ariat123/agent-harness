# B2B intent and signal prospecting

Use for the business named in the request, whether AFT Labs or a client. Establish whose offer is being marketed, who the customer is, and the desired outcome. For client work, use the client's facts, proof, voice, and operating constraints; do not inherit AFT offers, signatures, commercial terms, or campaign defaults. Load AFT business context only when AFT is the subject or its role in the requested work makes that context relevant.

For AFT work, read AFT context (private reference, not included). Identify seller, exact offer/outcome, target buyer, geography, and research objective before selecting signals. Use existing evidence where available; label untested signal ideas as hypotheses.

This skill stays B2B across industries. It can find accounts for AFT, business-owned property portfolios for a property manager, or corporate catering prospects for a restaurant. Consumer dining, individual renter acquisition, and person-level behavioral targeting are outside this account-prospecting workflow. Establish whether a property owner is an individual consumer or business account before applying B2B assumptions.

## Choose the research lane

Distinguish fit (could benefit), business trigger (an event), engagement (interaction with the seller), anonymous category research, and an explicit purchase request. A company signal does not identify which employee acted or prove budget, authority, urgency, or buying readiness.

- Keep suitable ICP accounts without signals in an ordinary ICP lane. Missing signals are not evidence of no demand.
- Require sourced relevant signals for a signal-based treatment. Inspect the actual event, not the vendor's generic hot-lead label.
- Qualify explicit requests for the category, decision process, deadline, and supplier eligibility. An irrelevant RFP is not a priority.

## Research and prioritize

1. Map the offer to a small library of observable events and why each might matter. Compare won and nonwinning accounts if data exists; do not require prior wins to start a hypothesis.
2. Use economical sources appropriate to the hypothesis: job descriptions, public filings/news/permits, relevant conversations, or available first-party/paid data. A public trigger cannot validate the lift of a different paid feed.
3. Verify entity match and retrieve the underlying source. Preserve source class, dates, factual excerpt, and uncertainty. Syndicated versions of one announcement count as one signal.
4. Evaluate evidence reliability, entity resolution, offer relevance, and current timing separately. Record alternative explanations. Prioritize an action with a rationale rather than inventing a buying probability.
5. Find relevant contacts separately from account scoring. In AFT batches, use the canonical Lead Ops task packet and verification gates; research does not certify an email address.
6. Write an angle from the observed fact or an authorized direct conversation. Explain relevance without asserting an internal pain, private browsing, or buyer identity you cannot establish.
7. Apply do-not-contact rules and campaign-specific routing separately. Preserve customers, recent losses, stale evidence, and holds with reasons instead of deleting records. Recontact depends on the recorded context.

For account blocks, lifecycle rules, vendor-data interpretation, or assessing contactability and client handoffs, read account evidence (private reference, not included). For selecting tools or measuring lift, read evaluation (private reference, not included).

## Output and handoff

Return the requested accounts with evidence, fit, priority rationale, unknowns, and a truthful angle. State collection coverage and failures. For an AFT batch, the emitted task-packet schema and complete accounting take priority over a convenience table.

For operational handoffs, distinguish a researched account, a credible contact route, confirmed interest, and a qualified attended conversation. Apply the client's agreed acceptance criteria and identify who owns the next action; do not silently change the requested service scope or payment terms.

Hand the buyer/offer/angle and its source to cold-email writing; do not embed sequences or email infrastructure into this skill. Research outputs do not authorize contacting accounts, buying data, or changing campaigns.
