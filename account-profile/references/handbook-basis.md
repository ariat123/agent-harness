# Where each design choice comes from

Every structural choice in this skill traces to a public GitLab handbook page. All pages accessed 2026-10-10.

| Choice in the skill | Handbook basis | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Draft only the Account Profile, and stop there | The account plan has four parts: Account Profile, Relationship and Influence Mapping, Whitespace Mapping, Action Plan. Step 2 of building a plan is "Build the Account Profile." | [Account Planning](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#components-of-an-account-plan) |
| Eight questions, in handbook order | The Account Profile section lists the questions to "start at a high level." | [Account Planning, Account Profile](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#account-profile) |
| About the customer, no GitLab pitch in questions 1 to 7 | "The account profile is all about the customer (not GitLab)." | [Account Planning, Account Profile](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#account-profile) |
| Skip firmographics beyond a snapshot | "Basic account details should already be entered into SalesForce." | [Account Planning, Account Profile](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#account-profile) |
| Map to at most two of four named value drivers | Question 1 asks which value drivers matter most and why. Command of the Message names four, which exist "even if GitLab doesn't." | [Command of the Message](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/command-of-the-message/#customer-value-drivers) |
| 10-Ks and filings first, then news and hiring | The account research table points to "Financial disclosures and 10-K reports" for "strategic priorities directly from leadership," plus company news and strategic hires. | [Sales Development](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/sales-development/) |
| Mark gaps instead of guessing | Step 2: "Identify and work on closing information gaps with your team." The Action Plan section: a core benefit of a plan is "to figure out what you don't know." | [Account Planning, Building the Account Plan](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#step-2-build-the-account-profile) |
| End with questions for the champion | Step 6 asks: "Have I verified my understanding with my champion?" | [Account Planning, Step 6](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#step-6-review-monitor-and-iterate) |
| Re-run quarterly and on major changes | "A quarterly cadence to review and update plans," and "personnel changes, mergers/aquisitions, and competitive threats may trigger immediate review." | [Account Planning, Step 6](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#step-6-review-monitor-and-iterate) |
| No personal names, even from public sources | Personal data from public sources stays Orange, and a data set with personal data is Orange as a whole. | [Data Classification Standard](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/security/policies_and_standards/data-classification-standard/) |
| Question 8 only from public customer stories or the AE | Customer contracts and non-public financial information are Orange. Claude "cannot access GitLab systems or CRM data directly." | [Data Classification Standard](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/security/policies_and_standards/data-classification-standard/), [Sales Development, Claude guide](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/sales-development/#claude-sales-dev-bdrsdr-user-guide) |
| Never use customer platform data | Customer's Data from the GitLab platform is Red, the most restricted tier. | [Data Classification Standard](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/security/policies_and_standards/data-classification-standard/) |
| Runs in Claude, Green by default | "Sharing up to Orange-level data has been approved" for Claude. | [Sales Development, Claude guide](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/sales-development/#claude-sales-dev-bdrsdr-user-guide) |
| New data sources need review | New vendors or systems accessing Orange or Red data need a TPRM review before approval. | [Third Party Risk Management](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/security/security-assurance/security-risk/third-party-risk-management/) |
| The AE reviews every line | GitLab's AI agent roster says agent output "is an action aid, not a replacement for rep judgment." | [Sales Development, RelevanceAI](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/sales-development/#relevanceai) |
| Pilot: each champion works up to three accounts | "Ideally, focus your efforts on building up to three account plans at one time." | [Account Planning, Step 1](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/account-planning/#step-1-account-prioritization) |
| Pilot measures at four levels | Field Enablement measures engagement, sentiment, observed behavior, and business outcome. | [Field Enablement, How We Measure Success](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/field-operations/field-enablement/#how-we-measure-success) |
| Pilot feedback in a GitLab issue template | Sales Development collects agent feedback through an issue template in a GitLab project. | [Sales Development](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/sales-development/) |
