# Account Profile: Deere & Company (NYSE: DE)

Draft for AE review. Built 2026-10-10 from public sources only. Data tier: Green.

**Snapshot:** Makes agricultural, turf, construction, and forestry equipment and finances it through John Deere Financial. Fiscal 2025 (ended November 2, 2025): net sales and revenues of $45,684 million, net income attributable to Deere of $5,027 million, about 73,100 employees. [S1]

## 1. Goals and strategic initiatives
- The strategy is the Smart Industrial Operating Model, built on three pillars: production systems, technology stack, and lifecycle solutions. [S1]
- In December 2025 the company refined its Leap Ambitions, multi-year goals that emphasize automation, autonomy, digitalization, lifecycle solutions, and Solutions as a Service (SaaS). [S1]
- In March 2026 the board approved one-time senior officer stock awards tied to the refined Leap Ambitions, with a performance period running to October 27, 2030. [S7]
- Read: software is now part of how Deere plans to grow, so how fast it ships digital products is a business issue, not only an IT one. [S1, S2]

**Value drivers most likely to matter**
1. Deliver Better Products Faster: the strategy rests on software-heavy products, and the 10-K lists falling behind in new technologies as a strategic risk. [S1]
2. Reduce Security and Compliance Risk: the 10-K flags connected-product security, data privacy and AI rules, and the July 2026 FTC settlement adds compliance reporting. [S1, S2]

## 2. Macro factors
- Weak farm markets in fiscal 2025 meant lower sales volumes, more sales incentives, and higher receivable write-offs. [S1]
- Direct tariff costs were about $600 million in fiscal 2025 and $502 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026, net of refunds after the February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling on IEEPA tariffs. [S1, S2]
- The company expects large ag to stay subdued in North America and soften in South America in 2026, with small ag and turf and construction and forestry improving. [S2]

## 3. Competitors and share
- Ag and turf: the 10-K names AGCO, CLAAS, CNH Industrial, Kubota, and Toro; construction and forestry rivals include Caterpillar, Komatsu, and Volvo Construction Equipment. [S1]
- The 10-K says ag is also drawing technology-focused companies and start-ups as competitors. [S1]
- To add technology, it bought Tenna, a construction mixed-fleet tracking company, in February 2026 for $439 million. Market share is not disclosed in the filings opened. [S2]

## 4. Biggest risks
- Short term: the bottom of the ag cycle. On August 20, 2026 the company raised its fiscal 2026 net income forecast to $4.75 billion to $5.00 billion, still below fiscal 2025, and said it expects 2026 to mark the bottom of the cycle. [S1, S3]
- Medium term: the December 2025 10-K reports slower than expected adoption of some precision technology and SaaS services; the August 2026 release cites increasing adoption. [S1, S3]
- Long term: the 10-K warns its business may suffer if it cannot integrate AI into processes, products, and services in a timely, compliant way. [S1]

## 5. How IT supports or hinders the strategy
- The John Deere Operations Center stores large data volumes at the edge and in cloud environments, and the 10-K calls reliance on third-party cloud systems a particular risk. [S1]
- In December 2025 the executive serving as CIO and President of John Deere Financial announced retirement effective January 31, 2026. As of the 10-K, the CISO reported to the CIO. [S1, S4]
- Job post hint: an August 10, 2026 senior software engineer post for a factory operations product asks the hire to integrate with enterprise platforms for data, identity, observability, and CI/CD. [S9]

## 6. Digital transformation strategy
- The technology stack pillar covers hardware, embedded software, connectivity, data platforms, and applications, shared across the enterprise. [S1]
- The December 2025 investor presentation shows 1M+ connected machines and about 500M engaged acres, with no as-of date on the slide. [S8]
- Inside the company, Deere uses generative AI in business processes and is adding AI-driven analytics and robotics to manufacturing. [S1]
